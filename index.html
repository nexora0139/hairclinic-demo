The consultation process

Four steps, before any decision is made.

Nothing here is designed to rush you. Each step exists so that if you do proceed, you're doing so with full information.

01

You reach out

Message us on WhatsApp with a few details — no forms, no commitment.

02

We schedule a visit

A time is arranged at the clinic for an in-person scalp assessment.

03

You get an honest read

The specialist explains what's actually happening, and what your realistic options are — including "not yet."

04

You decide, in your own time

If you'd like to proceed, pricing and timelines are laid out clearly. If not, that's a complete outcome too.

Our approach

Assessment first. Everything else follows from that.

  • Scalp

    A physical, in-person examination — not a judgement made from a photo you send online.

  • History

    Your medical history and hair loss pattern are reviewed before any option is discussed.

  • Options

    Where relevant, non-surgical paths are discussed alongside surgical ones — whichever fits your situation.

  • Pricing

    Costs are explained in full before you commit to anything, with no hidden follow-up charges.

Questions people ask before visiting

What to expect

Does the consultation cost anything?

The initial consultation is free. Any cost only applies if you choose to proceed with a recommended plan, and that will be explained clearly beforehand.

Will I be told I need a procedure regardless of my situation?

No. Some visitors are advised to monitor and return later, or referred toward non-surgical management. The assessment determines the recommendation, not the other way round.

Who actually conducts the consultation?

A registered medical practitioner at the clinic — not a sales counsellor.

How do I book a visit?

Message the clinic on WhatsApp using the button on this page, and a team member will help you find a time.

If you're unsure, the consultation is the place to find out.
